Asset provenance

The entry landscape, phone landscape, navigation atlas and eight portrait atlas are commissioned imagegen assets. Fonts are locally bundled Cormorant Garamond and Atkinson Hyperlegible under their supplied OFL licences. No generated lettering is used.

Exact prompts and source assets

Button focus, selected and disabled styles come from production CSS; motion comes from the shared feedback action.
