Allocate expectations

Distribute exactly 100 whole points across markets, training camps, bases and space stations. Zero is allowed. Editing these points spends no credits and changes no confirmed allocation. Apply focus requests an order; wait for acceptance.

Read the scheduled outcome

An accepted order records its application tick. Dividends use the allocation from the beginning of each tick, so a changed allocation first affects the following tick. Multiple orders due together apply in accepted order; the last allocation becomes current. Pauses delay progress. Reload recovers accepted orders.

Share a dividend pool

Other empires’ completed developments contribute 10% of their paid construction price to a shared pool. Each eligible system’s weight is its beginning-of-tick market count multiplied by its focus on that development type. The pool is divided by those weights; it is not a guaranteed credit per point. Your own builds never pay your markets.

Confirmed and held returns

New markets join dividend weights from the next tick. A system must retain its surface owner through settlement to receive a share. When orbit and surface are split, returns stay held at the system instead of becoming spendable credits. Read confirmed income and held credits separately; other players’ future building choices remain uncertain.

When it takes effect

Other empires’ completions create a shared dividend pool; a higher allocation increases your markets’ share for that type. Future purchases and returns are uncertain. The order applies at its next eligible tick. Dividends use the focus frozen at the beginning of that tick, so your new allocation first affects the following tick. Share a dividend pool

Other empires’ completed developments contribute 10% of their paid construction price to a shared pool. Each eligible system’s weight is its beginning-of-tick market count multiplied by its focus on that development type. The pool is divided by those weights; it is not a guaranteed credit per point. Your own builds never pay your markets.
