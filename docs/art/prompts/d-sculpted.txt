Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a polished landscape art-direction comparison board for Cluster II, a slow real-time space strategy game about diplomacy, interdependence and separate orbital and planetary control. This is original game concept art, not a UI mockup.
Layout: precise four-column by three-row grid, twelve equally sized illustrated panels, narrow consistent gutters, no text or lettering. Columns always aquatic M, humanoid G, insectoid A, reptilian O. Top row: four expressive head-and-shoulders faction leader portraits. Middle row: four distinct spacecraft in three-quarter view against quiet space. Bottom row: four planetary vistas seen from orbit with a visible surface settlement and a separate orbital installation. Show each subject clearly; use disciplined compositions.
Shared subject brief: column 1 aquatic diplomat with broad fin crest, dark reflective eyes, teal skin, pearl mantle and copper breathing collar; elegant crescent ship with pearl shell and teal details; ocean planet with island settlements beneath a red dwarf. Column 2 older dark-skinned human woman, short silver hair, ochre formal jacket, calm perceptive expression, visible face; practical ivory modular long-spine ship with ochre radiators; temperate planet with agricultural regions and orbital docks lit by a yellow star. Column 3 insectoid envoy with branching antennae, compound eyes, segmented violet chitin and ivory ceremonial collar, intelligent rather than monstrous; radial segmented ship with three paired spars, ivory and violet; pale mineral planet with geometric settlements and orbital lattice lit by a white star. Column 4 reptilian statesperson with broad brow, strong jaw, rust scales, jade ceremonial mantle, composed expression; broad wedge ship with terraced armor and jade details; arid planet with terraced cities and an orbital ring under blue starlight.
All four leaders have equal dignity, agency and visual prominence. No weapons in portraits. Alien anatomy remains distinct. The ships are spacecraft, not aircraft. Star-light colors are environmental illumination, not a mandatory faction palette. Preserve readable silhouettes and large value masses. No recognizable franchise designs, logos, watermarks, captions, numbers or labels.
STYLE DIRECTION: Highly stylized handmade claymation diorama, visibly sculpted colored clay and glazed ceramic. All twelve panels MUST look like hand-built stop-motion miniatures, boldly different from cinematic realism. Leader heads have large simplified facial planes, expressive sculpted eyes, chunky rounded forms and reduced detail. The human is a stylized clay puppet with silver hair sculpted as a few large curls, no individual hair strands, no skin pores. Alien fins, antennae and scales are large simple sculpted shapes. Ships are chunky elegant clay miniatures with only a few clear component forms, no tiny mechanical greebles. Planet scenes are handcrafted miniature ceramic globes with raised island or continent shapes, tiny simplified settlements, curved horizon and separate orbital stations. Soft bright studio lighting, muted pastel turquoise, ochre, lavender and terracotta, visible fingerprints and subtle clay tool marks. Strong readable shapes and generous quiet backgrounds. No photorealism, no cinematic realism, no realistic skin, no intricate metallic textures, no film-grain realism. Maintain dignified diplomatic expressions, no slapstick, no packaging.
