# Research selection

The Economy instrument offers all eight research tracks with current levels, retained science and descriptions that distinguish implemented production effects from later intelligence, travel and combat interactions. A selection sends an encrypted command through the existing authenticated ingress, exact receipt binding and canonical transaction. Pending, conflict and connection remedies preserve the selected track; a receipt confirms admission, while the private queue names the effective tick. Tick publication records the applied selections and science outcome together.

The shared reducer assigns a monotonic sequence from the canonical revision. Admission strictly before the next boundary targets that tick; admission at or after it targets the first future boundary. Admission time is sampled after authentication/decryption inside the transaction and recomputed on transaction retry; client receipt-delivery time does not determine cutoff. Trigger delays do not backdate admission. Catch-up cannot redirect newly admitted science into historical ticks. Ordered selections preserve unfinished progress and transfer a capped track's bank only when switching away from it. Production still uses beginning-of-tick levels, and science completes levels afterwards. Pauses shift the clock while preserving effective tick numbers. Existing schema-1 campaigns remain unchanged and cannot submit research orders.

Queues and outcomes are included only in the owning member's encrypted projection/history. No client-supplied actor identity or authoritative time is accepted. Each player can have at most 64 outstanding research orders; admission rejects additional orders explicitly and preserves existing ones. This is a development operating budget for the encrypted document, not owner-approved balance or a claim of measured public capacity. Research formulas and the existing rule proposals remain under review. Public campaigns remain closed.

The production research component also appears in the gallery and animation lab. Lab frames use the actual selection and production reducers, shared receipt copy and feedback animation, with replay, seek, speed and reduced-motion comparison.
