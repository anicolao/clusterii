# Campaign entry and invitation recovery

Continue appears only for an account with a persisted campaign membership. One membership opens that campaign directly; multiple memberships open the private directory. Open campaigns retains access to the directory and new campaigns. Your identity opens the existing editor and restores keyboard focus when closed. The entry uses the production illustrated controls and separate phone composition.

A signed-out campaign URL carries its canonical campaign ID through the entry and Google sign-in. Saving identity returns to that campaign after the authoritative receipt accepts the save. An existing saved identity can open the requested campaign directly. The return path accepts only canonical IDs and constructs an internal, base-aware route; arbitrary redirect targets are rejected. Campaign authorization still determines what the recipient may see or join.

The account observes its own membership index only after developer access is verified. Read failures have an explicit reconnect action; subscriptions and callbacks are scoped to the current account. Public games remain closed while rules and real cryptography are unfinished.

This is a campaign-entry increment for milestones 1/3/10. Emulator Google sign-in proves the application journey, not real Google account login on the hosted preview. Independent rendered review and full CI remain acceptance gates. No baseline images are regenerated.
