# Private credit offers

Economy → Credit offers lets a commander name a counterpart and exact credit amounts, review the reservation, accept or withdraw, and inspect the confirmed outcome. A zero return side is a gift. The commitment rail keeps the available balance and current action visible while details scroll. Escape restores the opening control. The production component also appears in the gallery and deterministic animation lab, including pending, interrupted, accepted and settled states with equal reduced-motion outcomes.

This implements the credit-only portion of RULES_SPEC §8 for development campaigns. The eight-tick expiry, tick-phase settlement and first-admitted acceptance/withdrawal ordering remain review proposals, not new owner-approved gameplay rules. Observation-report exchanges and alliances remain incomplete. No stars, forces, research, future promises or market dividends enter an offer.

## Stored compatibility and service contract

New campaigns store `creditOffersVersion: 1` and an empty private offer ledger. Earlier campaigns retain their recorded economy, history and access. Their economy explains that credit offers require a new development campaign; no historical reservations or transfers are reconstructed.

Issuer funds are reserved on offer admission. Acceptance reserves the counterpart's exact return amount. Only the named counterpart accepts; only the issuer withdraws. Immutable command receipts and canonical revision fencing prevent retries or concurrent acceptance from reserving twice. The first admitted acceptance or withdrawal locks the offer. Both sides settle at phase 3, ordered by admitted sequence, or both reservations return. Expiry precedes settlement at the beginning of the expiry tick. Scheduler catch-up executes intermediate ticks, so an accepted offer settles at its recorded tick rather than expiring merely because the worker ran late. Pause stops ticks and new trade admission; migration preserves outstanding terms and reservations.

Credits use exact integer milliunits. Settlement preflights both wallets, including other outstanding reservations. An unrepresentable incoming exchange fails atomically and refunds both sides. Production cannot consume numeric headroom required to return reservations; an invalid numeric state rejects the entire canonical transaction rather than partially crediting an empire. Pending storage is bounded to 64 offers per campaign, with capacity reserved in the existing canonical snapshot budget. This operational bound is not a gameplay balance approval.

Canonical terms contain account identities; recipient projections explicitly replace them with public seat indices and include only the viewer's offers/outcomes. No unrelated participant receives terms, opposing wallet balances or usable private history grants. The development mock provider exercises the same service and membership checks without a confidentiality claim. Real mode uses the existing authenticated encrypted command, canonical snapshot and per-recipient history protocol.
