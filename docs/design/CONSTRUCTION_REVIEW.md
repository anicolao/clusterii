# System construction

Develop opens an illustrated construction instrument for the selected owned system. Four distinct scenes identify Market, Training camp, Base and Space station. The current quote, available balance and resulting balance accompany a persistent Build/Back commitment rail; capacity, layer ownership, paused state and insufficient funds explain why a build is unavailable. Each layer shows its own queue, progress, cancellation status and settled outcomes. Phone details scroll inside the instrument while its commitment remains visible. Labels and controls are semantic UI; art is decorative, never hidden intelligence.

## Authority and settlement

Encrypted build and cancellation commands use canonical revisions, authenticated membership, layer entitlement and exact receipt binding. Admission reserves the authoritative quote once. Price uses exact integer arithmetic for `ceil(base × (23/20)^count)`; count includes completed and already accepted orders of that type. A concurrent stale purchase fails without spending. Per-layer queue and per-type terraforming capacity are checked server-side. Client time, price and owner fields are rejected.

Admission before a boundary targets that tick; at/after it targets the first future tick, including during catch-up. At execution, ownership and capacity are revalidated. A pre-execution invalid order returns its full reservation. An eligible order starts at its layer's turn, then takes four advances starting on the following tick. A successor starts on its predecessor's completion tick and does not advance immediately. Production uses newly completed developments that tick and beginning-of-tick research. Pause preserves tick numbers and the remaining clock interval.

Cancellation executes in admitted sequence at its assigned tick. It returns the full reservation before progress or half after progress, with no speculative dividend. Captured executed queues return nothing. The UI distinguishes an accepted cancellation from its settled refund. The same lease/revision transaction publishes the complete construction outcome, production ledger, balances, encrypted history and member projections, so duplicate worker delivery cannot repeat a completion or payout.

Each completion creates a bounded pool of 10% of the actual paid quote. Beginning-of-tick markets qualify only if their surface ownership persists; the builder is excluded. Stable largest-remainder allocation conserves the pool, and split ownership sends shares to local escrow. Newly completed markets join dividend weights next tick. Dividends receive no extra investing multiplier. This slice uses the ruleset's existing equal 25/25/25/25 focus; editable focus remains the next market interaction, not an implemented feature.

Build queues and outcomes appear only in their owner's private view; a live queue also requires current entitlement to its layer. Opposing layer values are not spread into projections. No public search metadata contains quotes, queues or outcomes. Schema-1 games cannot construct. Schema-2 games gain orders only through explicit player actions; opening this preview does not migrate a campaign.

Implementing them in development games does not approve them. Public admission stays closed. Capture tests exercise the specified reducer boundary; no unavailable capture action is presented in the player UI.
